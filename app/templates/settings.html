Create a key at account.arena.net/applications with exactly these permissions checked: account, wallet, progression, unlocks, characters, builds, inventories. Nothing else is needed, and the GW2 API is read-only end to end — it has no endpoint that can move items, spend currency, or otherwise act on your account, so this key can only let this site display your account info, never touch it. See exactly what each permission is for on the right.

Disclaimer

Your key is encrypted at rest, not stored in plain text — but that encryption is reversible (the site has to decrypt it to make API calls on your behalf), so whoever runs this server technically holds the means to decrypt it, same as any self-hosted tool without end-to-end encryption. What no one can ever do with it, regardless of who holds the key: act on your account in any way — there's no way to spend your gold, trade or move your items, send mail, or anything else, because the GW2 API itself has no endpoint capable of any of that.
